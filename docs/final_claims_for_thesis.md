# Final claims for thesis drafting

Freeze note: the claim classifications below are the scientific v1 interpretation. The earlier pre-freeze wording records when this synthesis was first prepared; presentation-only edits must preserve the substance and cite the frozen machine-readable results.

This is a pre-freeze scientific synthesis of existing results, not new estimation. The frozen primary outcome is the natural logarithm of the *listed* nightly price for 12,412 conventional entire-home listings in the Copenhagen--Frederiksberg urban core. DKK is user-confirmed; the source price string does not encode a currency code. Phase 9 M0/ME/MW/MEW are the **Base specification**. M0_CTX/ME_CTX/MW_CTX/MEW_CTX are the **Area-context-adjusted core specification**: chronologically a **Post-Phase-9 implementation of a planned contextual specification**, not the original frozen Phase 7 model. RQ2 uses both on the same common sample and saved folds; see the [core comparison panel](../outputs/tables/final/table_rq2_area_context.csv) and [core RQ2 figure](../outputs/figures/context_incremental_accessibility.png). The seven formal judgements and machine-readable evidence are in [`outputs/tables/conclusion_stability.csv`](../outputs/tables/conclusion_stability.csv). All predictive gains below are reductions in out-of-fold RMSE of log listed price, not causal effects.

## SAFE TO CLAIM

- **RQ1:** An 800 m straight-line circle and a 10-minute walking-network budget describe empirically different access to the same canonical destinations: food/social and cultural/tourist opportunity counts differ for 99.4% and 82.0% of the common sample, despite high correlation. This is a measurement distinction, **not** evidence that walking measures predict price better. [Matched-measure audit](../outputs/tables/phase10/accessibility_representation.csv); [final accessibility definitions](../outputs/tables/final/table4_accessibility_definitions.csv).
- **RQ2, Base specification:** Phase 9 M0 contains listing, host/rental, municipality and City Hall centrality controls. Adding Euclidean ME or walking MW modestly improves geographic XGBoost prediction by 0.00482 or 0.00301 log-price RMSE, respectively, on the same 12,412 listings and saved holdouts. The positive increments are not uniform across districts or every geographic robustness design. [Core comparison panel](../outputs/tables/final/table_rq2_area_context.csv); [frozen performance](../outputs/tables/phase09/model_performance.csv); [fold performance](../outputs/tables/phase09/fold_performance.csv); [buffer comparison](../outputs/tables/final/table_validation_performance.csv).
- **RQ2, Area-context-adjusted core specification:** Adding log gross-polygon population density and disposable income to the base controls makes the independent ME/MW geographic XGBoost increments weak or unstable (+0.00004/−0.00056 relative to M0_CTX). This is a core RQ2 result, not merely an optional robustness footnote. It does not replace the frozen Phase 9 models or make the mixed Copenhagen district/Frederiksberg municipality context fully harmonised. [Core comparison panel](../outputs/tables/final/table_rq2_area_context.csv); [core RQ2 figure](../outputs/figures/context_incremental_accessibility.png); [source audit](../reports/postcompletion_01_context_audit.md).
- **RQ3:** The saved random five-fold split is a spatially interspersed benchmark; the 11-group leave-one-official-area-out (LOAO) design is the primary unseen-area geographic test. The saved 1.5 km whole-block five-fold split is non-administrative robustness, and LOAO plus 500/1,000 m training exclusions tests adjacency/separation. All ten Copenhagen districts and Frederiksberg municipality are test data once; no one district was selected at random. [Validation methods table](../outputs/tables/final/table_validation_designs.csv); [saved-fold visualisation audit](../reports/pre_freeze_validation_visualisation.md); [fold metrics](../outputs/tables/phase09/fold_performance.csv).
- **RQ3 performance:** Geographic separation changes prediction error: frozen XGBoost MW RMSE is 0.31205 under random CV, 0.32263 under 1.5 km blocks, 0.32976 under LOAO, and 0.33629/0.33965 under 500/1,000 m buffered LOAO. These schemes answer different prediction questions; their error differences cannot all be assigned to leakage, because held-out geography, training size and predictor support also change. [Saved validation-performance summary](../outputs/tables/final/table_validation_performance.csv); [Phase 8 support audit](../reports/phase08_eda_and_cv.md); [Phase 10 buffer diagnostics](../reports/phase10_robustness_and_extensions.md).
- **Boundary and price-observability checks:** Restricting destination *endpoints* to the Copenhagen/Frederiksberg union leaves the substantive M0-to-ME/MW and Euclidean-versus-walking ordering broadly intact, while walking paths remain free to cross administrative borders. Keeping only high-price-completeness dates retains 11,808 listings and broadly preserves the original baseline accessibility and random/geographic patterns. Neither check repairs possible missing-price selection. [Boundary comparison](../outputs/tables/boundary_sensitivity_comparison.csv); [price-observability comparison](../outputs/tables/price_observability_comparison.csv); [observability audit](../outputs/tables/price_observability_by_scrape.csv).

## CLAIM WITH QUALIFICATION

- **Area socioeconomic context limitations:** The adjusted core comparison was conceptually planned but not estimable at the Phase 7 freeze because the Copenhagen/Frederiksberg sources were not yet reconciled. It was implemented after Phase 9, on the same mixed-area sample. Copenhagen-only MW_CTX gains +0.00091 but improves only five of ten districts. Thus the original base-specification gain is **not stable evidence of additional information beyond observed area socioeconomic context**. Frederiksberg is one municipality-level proxy; City and national income denominators may differ; polygon density has unverified boundary vintage and water treatment. Treat contextual OLS associations as exploratory area-level evidence, not harmonised neighbourhood or listing-level effects. [Core panel](../outputs/tables/final/table_rq2_area_context.csv); [Copenhagen-only sensitivity](../outputs/tables/copenhagen_only_context_sensitivity.csv); [context-source audit](../reports/postcompletion_01_context_audit.md); [context-model report](../reports/postcompletion_02_context_models.md).
- **Within-area explanatory associations:** Primary HC3 OLS coefficients describe conditional associations, not causal amenity effects. Area fixed effects attenuate the cultural coefficient and reverse the food point estimate; a single spatial-error sensitivity preserves broad coefficient directions. HC3 alone does not correct residual spatial clustering or the small number of independent context units. [Frozen coefficient table](../outputs/tables/phase09/ols_coefficients.csv); [Phase 10 association robustness](../reports/phase10_robustness_and_extensions.md); [context-model inference caveat](../reports/postcompletion_02_context_models.md).
- **External validity:** Price is missing/invalid for 8,559 of 21,080 otherwise eligible conventional entire homes, concentrated strongly by scrape date. The high-completeness restriction supports the observed-price pattern, but does not establish that missing prices are random or generalisable to unpriced listings. The September OSM snapshot postdates the June/July Airbnb scrape and listing coordinates are approximate. [Scrape-batch table](../outputs/tables/price_observability_by_scrape.csv); [observability report](../reports/postcompletion_03_price_observability.md); [Phase 9 limitations](../reports/phase09_main_models.md).

## DO NOT CLAIM

- Do not claim walking-network accessibility meaningfully or consistently outperforms the matched Euclidean measures. At the primary threshold, geographic XGBoost MW RMSE is 0.32976 versus ME 0.32795; threshold and union-endpoint sensitivities do not establish a stable walking advantage. [Frozen performance](../outputs/tables/phase09/model_performance.csv); [threshold robustness](../reports/phase10_robustness_and_extensions.md); [boundary comparison](../outputs/tables/boundary_sensitivity_comparison.csv).
- Do not claim a stable incremental price-prediction gain *beyond observed area socioeconomic context*, or describe the mixed-area fields as harmonised neighbourhood measures. The area-context-adjusted core ME/MW geographic gains are near zero or negative, and the mixed-resolution sources retain comparability caveats. [Core RQ2 panel](../outputs/tables/final/table_rq2_area_context.csv); [context audit](../reports/postcompletion_01_context_audit.md).
- Do not claim that every random-versus-spatial CV gap quantifies spatial leakage, that Frederiksberg is an eleventh Copenhagen district, or that whole 1.5 km blocks guarantee a 1.5 km train--test gap. [Validation design report](../reports/pre_freeze_validation_visualisation.md); [geographic support diagnostics](../reports/phase08_eda_and_cv.md).
- Do not claim the model estimates realised bookings, occupancy, transaction prices, bus *service* accessibility, or GTFS multimodal travel. The outcome is an observed listed nightly price; simple bus-stop proximity is a separate secondary robustness proxy, and approved GTFS was not available. [Phase 9 report](../reports/phase09_main_models.md); [Phase 10 transport report](../reports/phase10_robustness_and_extensions.md); [pre-analysis specification](preanalysis_specification.md).
