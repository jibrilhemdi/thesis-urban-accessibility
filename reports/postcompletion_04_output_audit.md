## QA and scientific limitations

- **Units and labels:** listed price is user-confirmed DKK/night; log price is the natural log of listed DKK price. OLS coefficients and predictive errors are in log-price units. Metre/km, walking minutes, 500 m squares and EPSG:25832 are explicit. M0/ME/MW/MEW notation is unchanged.
- **Denominators:** Tables 3, 5–7 and Figures 2–3/5–9 use or summarise the frozen 12,412-row primary common model sample, except Figure 8 naturally displays its 11 fold partitions. Table 1 additionally shows pre-price and pre-access stages. Table 8 deliberately shows subset-specific Ns, and Figure 4 counts public POIs, not listings. Different-denominator robustness rows are not ranked by their raw RMSE against Phase 9.
- **Context qualification:** the v2 proposal requests neighbourhood density and income in the main baseline, but the audited sources are not fully harmonised. The frozen Phase 9 M0 therefore excludes them. Table 2/3 describe the later, provisional 11-area context and Table 8 names its **post-completion robustness** result; Frederiksberg remains one municipality proxy with a different income definition. The output package does not rewrite that history.
- **Privacy:** all final exported tables are aggregate; no listing IDs, host names, free text or row-level Airbnb coordinates appear. Figures 2–3 suppress cells with fewer than five listings; Figure 4 uses a public OSM network node. Figure 8 uses official polygons. Internal private coordinates are read only to aggregate maps/correlations.
- **Figure reuse:** Figures 6, 7 and 9 are byte-for-byte copies of existing code-generated phase outputs. Figures 1–5 and 8 are newly generated from saved inputs/PostGIS. Existing source figures remain in their phase folders. Reuse of a frozen result is not a new statistical estimate.
- **Outstanding manuscript judgement:** Tables 2, 3, 5 and 8 need width/length handling in the thesis document (e.g. landscape pages or concise footnotes); Figure 9 can move to appendix if the main-text page budget is tight. That is formatting, not missing analysis. Full-sample explanatory OLS standard errors address heteroskedasticity but not all spatial dependence; geographic error reflects both separation and area-specific extrapolation. OSM extraction postdates the Airbnb snapshot.
