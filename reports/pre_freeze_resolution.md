## 2. Validation design fixed for thesis wording

Random five-fold is the **spatially interspersed prediction benchmark**. LOAO is the **primary reported unseen-area geographic generalisation test**. The saved 1.5 km whole-block five-fold split is a **non-administrative spatial robustness** design: all listings in one square share a fold, and multiple squares form one fold. LOAO with 500/1,000 m EPSG:25832 training buffers is **adjacency/separation robustness**, with the original test polygons unchanged. There are exactly **11** LOAO test groups: ten Copenhagen districts and Frederiksberg municipality. The saved Phase 9 XGBoost MW [fold table](../outputs/tables/phase09/fold_performance.csv) contains 11 distinct geographic test identifiers whose Ns sum to 12,412; every area is tested once and no single district was randomly chosen. The original Phase 7 1 km outer-grid assignment still exists but is not relabelled as the Phase 9 reported main test. [Validation-design table](../outputs/tables/final/table_validation_designs.csv); [validation report](pre_freeze_validation_visualisation.md); [deviation chronology](../docs/deviations_from_preanalysis.md).

## 3. Accessibility-boundary design fixed for thesis wording

The **primary** measure uses the buffered canonical destination universe; nearby POIs/stations beyond the outer Copenhagen--Frederiksberg line can legitimately be reached. The **sensitivity** restricts destination *endpoints* to the official two-municipality union in EPSG:25832 (`ST_Covers`). District boundaries **never** restrict accessibility, and the walking graph/routes are **not clipped** to administrative borders. On the identical 12,412-listing sample and saved folds, geographic XGBoost ME/MW RMSE gains versus M0 change from +0.004824/+0.003009 (buffered) to +0.004926/+0.003383 (union endpoints). Geographic Euclidean remains lower-error than walking. This does **not materially change** the primary boundary conclusion or justify promoting the sensitivity. [Boundary report](pre_freeze_boundary_sensitivity.md); [machine-readable comparison](../outputs/tables/boundary_sensitivity_comparison.csv).

## 4. Research-question synthesis

- **RQ1:** Euclidean and walking-network measures are empirically distinct representations of access to the same destinations: at the primary 800 m/10-minute pair, food counts differ for 99.4% and cultural counts for 82.0% of common-sample listings, despite strong correlation. This establishes a measurement contrast, not walking-network predictive superiority. [Representation table](../outputs/tables/phase10/accessibility_representation.csv); [frozen model performance](../outputs/tables/phase09/model_performance.csv).
- **RQ2:** Evaluate both core comparisons on the same saved sample and folds. In the **Base specification** (listing, host/rental, municipality and City Hall centrality), accessibility modestly improves geographic XGBoost prediction: ME +0.00482 and MW +0.00301 RMSE reduction. In the **Area-context-adjusted core specification** (base plus log population density and disposable income), the corresponding gains are only +0.00004 and −0.00056; Copenhagen-only MW_CTX is +0.00091 but helps five of ten held-out districts. Thus a stable additional predictive contribution beyond observed area socioeconomic context is **not supported**, and walking-network access does not consistently outperform Euclidean access. The context estimates were implemented after Phase 9, not frozen at Phase 7; Copenhagen district and Frederiksberg municipality context have different geographic resolution and provisional source comparability. This scientific hierarchy does not rewrite Phase 9. [Core RQ2 panel](../outputs/tables/final/table_rq2_area_context.csv); [core RQ2 figure](../outputs/figures/context_incremental_accessibility.png); [matched source comparison](../outputs/tables/context_comparison.csv); [Copenhagen-only context](../outputs/tables/copenhagen_only_context_sensitivity.csv).
- **RQ3:** Frozen XGBoost MW RMSE is 0.31205 random, 0.32263 in the 1.5 km geometric blocks, 0.32976 LOAO, and 0.33629/0.33965 for 500/1,000 m buffered LOAO. The ordering is consistent with harder geographically separated prediction, while the original MW increment weakens to −0.00056/+0.00026 under the buffers. Do **not** attribute every gap to leakage alone: different held-out regions, excluded nearby training listings, sample support and Frederiksberg category extrapolation also matter. [Validation performance](../outputs/tables/final/table_validation_performance.csv); [Phase 8 support diagnostics](phase08_eda_and_cv.md); [Phase 10 buffer diagnostics](phase10_robustness_and_extensions.md).

Walking is **not** established as meaningfully better than Euclidean access (frozen geographic XGBoost MW RMSE 0.32976 versus ME 0.32795). The post-completion price-observability and endpoint-boundary checks preserve the qualified original patterns; neither proves absence of outcome-selection bias or removes the context attenuation. [Final claim matrix](../outputs/tables/conclusion_stability.csv); [price-observability report](postcompletion_03_price_observability.md); [boundary report](pre_freeze_boundary_sensitivity.md).
