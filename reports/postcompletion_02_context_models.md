# Post-completion 02 — context-augmented accessibility models

Date: 2026-09-29. **Status: post-completion context robustness, not a revision of frozen Phase 9 primary results.** The prior [context audit](postcompletion_01_context_audit.md) is the source of the two new area attributes. No primary output, fold, outcome, sample rule, 800 m/10-minute threshold, station/POI destination set, or Phase 9 estimator/tuning choice was replaced.

## Design and comparability

`analysis.analysis_dataset_context_v1` was joined on `(snapshot_date, listing_id)` to the saved `analysis.cv_assignments`. The mixed comparison uses **exactly the same 12,412 listings** as Phase 9: conventional entire homes with positive listed DKK price, official area, and reachable walking station. All 12,412 have both context fields. No price was imputed. The 11-area contextual table contributes only `log_analysis_area_population_density` (people per gross EPSG:25832 polygon km²) and `analysis_area_income_100k_dkk` (2024 mean disposable income in units of 100,000 DKK). The original municipality category and City Hall distance remain. No area fixed effects, proxy flag or income-definition flag enter the model matrix; those flags remain diagnostic metadata. M0_CTX, ME_CTX, MW_CTX and MEW_CTX append this same context block to their respective Phase 9 feature sets. MEW and MEW_CTX remain collinearity-sensitive exploratory combinations of highly correlated straight-line and walking measures.

The full-sample semilog OLS estimates use training-style median plus missingness indicator preprocessing and HC3 heteroskedasticity-robust intervals, exactly as the Phase 9 explanatory fit. The sole material predictor gap is the previously observed 123 missing bedrooms; the database retains NULLs. Predictive OLS and XGBoost use the **saved** five random and eleven leave-one-area-out folds, with all learned imputation/encoding fitted inside each training split. XGBoost reuses the three Phase 9 regularised candidate configurations, four municipality-stratified, 1-km-block-grouped **inner training-only folds**, seeds 20260929/20260930, and the same RMSE scoring. The held-out outcomes did not select variables, thresholds, preprocessing or parameters. The [metadata](../outputs/tables/context_augmented_metadata.json) and [inner-tuning table](../outputs/tables/context_augmented_tuning.csv) record the exact implementation.

The original Phase 9 metrics are compared only against the **identical 12,412 observations**. The separate Copenhagen-only sensitivity has **11,108** listings, omits Frederiksberg's 1,304, preserves the ten saved district fold labels, and is compared *within* that sample—not directly against the 12,412-row Phase 9 pooled RMSE as though samples matched. No district is merged. The smallest Copenhagen test fold has 221 listings.

## Explanatory OLS associations

In mixed-area M0_CTX, the density coefficient is **+0.0867** (HC3 95% CI **+0.0675 to +0.1058**) per one-unit increase in log density; income is **+0.2379** (CI **+0.2169 to +0.2588**) per 100,000 DKK higher area mean. In MW_CTX they remain positive, **+0.0727** (CI +0.0520 to +0.0934) and **+0.2182** (CI +0.1922 to +0.2441). M0_CTX adjusted in-sample R² is **0.4843**, versus Phase 9 M0's approximately **0.457**; use held-out metrics below for transferable prediction. In Copenhagen-only M0_CTX, density is **+0.0877** (CI +0.0683 to +0.1071) and income **+0.2410** (CI +0.2198 to +0.2622); MW_CTX yields +0.0756 and +0.2286. Thus both fields show positive **conditional in-sample associations**, not established individual effects or causal price determinants.

Context addition substantially changes the accessibility associations. Phase 9 ME's rail-distance coefficient is **−0.0557** log points/km, food **−0.0275**, cultural/tourist **+0.0822**; in ME_CTX these become **−0.0272**, **−0.0027** and **+0.0267**. Phase 9 MW's rail-walking coefficient is **−0.00154** per minute, food **−0.0207**, cultural/tourist **+0.0705**; in MW_CTX they are **−0.00092**, **+0.00966**, **+0.00869**. The MW_CTX food and cultural HC3 intervals cross zero, as does the walking-time interval; ME_CTX retains a smaller positive cultural association. Copenhagen-only MW_CTX has station **−0.00149**, food **+0.0112**, culture **+0.00261**. These changes are material, especially for cultural access, but do **not** show mediation or causality. Full coefficient, HC3 SE/CI, N, adjusted R², AIC/BIC and condition numbers are in [the OLS table](../outputs/tables/context_augmented_ols.csv).

**Inference warning:** these contextual values vary across only **11 mixed-resolution areas** (or ten City districts). HC3 handles heteroskedastic listing residuals, **not** area clustering or the small number of independent context units. The reported CIs can be too narrow for the contextual coefficients, even though 12,412 listings enter the fit. Density uses gross assignment-polygon areas with an unverified City WFS vintage and water treatment; City and national income denominators are not proven equivalent. Interpret signs and magnitudes as exploratory area-level associations, not statistically established neighbourhood effects. Area fixed effects are deliberately absent: they absorb these area-constant context variables and target within-area rather than between-area associations; the distinct Phase 10 fixed-effect robustness remains separate.

| OLS | MW | +0.00393 | **−0.00185** |
| OLS | MEW | +0.00567 | **−0.00151** |

For mixed-area XGBoost, M0_CTX geographic RMSE is **0.32675**; ME_CTX **0.32670**, MW_CTX **0.32731**, MEW_CTX **0.32574**. The original M0/ME/MW/MEW geographic RMSEs are **0.33277/0.32795/0.32976/0.32777**. The absolute context-model scores are often lower, but **accessibility's increment beyond the context-adjusted baseline is close to zero or negative**. That is the relevant question; a better score for a larger model does not promote it into frozen Phase 9. MEW_CTX's +0.00100 gain is modest and comes from a collinearity-sensitive combined block; it does not establish separate walking value. Random-fold context gains are also smaller than Phase 9: XGBoost ME/MW/MEW gains are +0.00179/+0.00144/+0.00197, versus +0.00902/+0.00840/+0.01009 without context. Geographic error remains above random error for every context XGBoost set, so random splitting remains optimistic.

## Paired geographic-fold stability

The [paired area table](../outputs/tables/context_augmented_geographic_pairs.csv) gives every area's N and original/context M0, ME and MW RMSEs for both estimators; the [fold table](../outputs/tables/context_augmented_fold_performance.csv) gives train/test N plus RMSE, MAE and R². Under XGBoost, ME improves in **6/11** context-adjusted areas (7/11 originally); MW improves in only **4/11** (7/11 originally). Its median area gain changes from about **+0.00271** to **−0.00039**. The pooled near-zero result is therefore not a uniform small improvement: Østerbro still gains +0.00640 with MW_CTX, while Vesterbro-Kongens Enghave loses −0.00616 and Vanløse loses −0.00634. Indre By changes from a +0.01170 original MW gain to −0.00249 with context. Every held-out area's context pair is absent as an exact *area combination* in training, even though individual values may fall inside training ranges. Frederiksberg changes from −0.00339 to +0.00199, but this is the held-out **municipality proxy** whose income source and municipality category are unseen in City-only training; it is an extrapolation stress test, not evidence that the sources were harmonised. OLS MW improves in 5/11 context-adjusted areas versus 6/11 originally.

## Copenhagen-only harmonisation sensitivity

On 11,108 Copenhagen listings and ten saved district held-outs, XGBoost geographic M0_CTX/ME_CTX/MW_CTX RMSE is **0.32820/0.32788/0.32729**. ME adds **+0.00031**, MW **+0.00091** RMSE reduction to the *Copenhagen-only context baseline*. MW improves in **5/10** districts and reverses in five; its small pooled gain does not establish broadly stable incremental value. All three OLS associations and XGBoost RMSE/MAE/R² are in [the sensitivity table](../outputs/tables/copenhagen_only_context_sensitivity.csv); detailed OLS coefficients and district fold metrics are in the general OLS/fold tables. This removes the cross-provider Frederiksberg income comparison and municipality-scale proxy, **not** the City polygon-vintage/water caveat or the fact that City income and population refer to different years.

## Answers and thesis disposition

1. **Population density and income:** both have positive conditional OLS coefficients in mixed and Copenhagen-only fits. Their apparent precision should not be treated as area-level inference with only 11/10 units and HC3 listing-level SEs; income comparability is unresolved in the mixed fit.
2. **Accessibility coefficients:** yes, they materially attenuate or change sign after context; the large Phase 9 cultural association nearly disappears in MW_CTX.
3. **Euclidean and walking prediction:** after context, mixed-area geographic XGBoost ME adds only +0.00004 RMSE reduction and MW adds −0.00056; OLS increments are negative. There is no persuasive mixed-area incremental geographic gain from either alone. Copenhagen-only MW has a small +0.00091 gain, but only half the districts improve.
4. **Geographic-CV conclusion:** the Phase 9 accessibility increment is **not robust** to this two-variable contextual adjustment. MEW_CTX retains a small exploratory gain, not a reversal of the main scientific caution. Geographic validation remains harder than random validation.
5. **Thesis claim:** it is reasonable to say accessibility is a distinct measured representation and that it improved the original Phase 9 baseline modestly. It is **not** reasonable, on these results, to claim a stable additional predictive contribution **beyond observed area socioeconomic context**, nor that walking consistently outperforms Euclidean access. The contextual model is exploratory because the mixed geography/income and polygon-area definitions remain imperfect; no causal conclusion follows.
