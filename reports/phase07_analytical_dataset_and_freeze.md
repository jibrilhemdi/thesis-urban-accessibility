# Phase 7 — analytical dataset, missingness and pre-analysis freeze

Completed 2026-09-29, **before any price-model fitting**. Reproduce with `make phase7-run` and `THESIS_PHASE7_TEST=1 make phase7-test`. PostgreSQL/PostGIS is authoritative; the CSVs and figure listed below are aggregate exports. Migrations `011_analysis_dataset_v1.sql` and `012_phase7_spatial_cv_folds.sql` create the listing view and persisted location-only fold freeze. No raw source file or existing NULL was changed.

## Dataset construction and QA

`analysis.analysis_dataset_v1` LEFT JOINs `clean.airbnb_listings`, `features.listing_spatial_base`, `features.euclidean_accessibility`, and `features.walking_accessibility` on **(snapshot_date, listing_id)**. Each parent feature relation has its own unique composite key; the run checks all 23,144 rows and distinct keys. It has **80 documented fields**; see [the generated dictionary](../docs/data_dictionary_analysis.csv). Raw `beds` is read separately for redundancy diagnosis, joined to the same source file ID and original listing ID, but does not enter the model view or primary controls. Price is the single positive source listed price for each listing, never substituted by scrape batch. The numeric value is treated as DKK/night under user confirmation, while the source does not itself declare a currency code.

| Population / restriction | N |
|---|---:|
| All source listings, retained in the view | 23,144 |
| Positive observed source price, any listing type | 13,860 |
| Missing/invalid source price, any listing type | 9,284 (40.11%) |
| Conventional entire homes before price selection | 21,080 |
| Phase 2 priced primary candidates | 12,521 |
| Primary candidates with unique official CV-area assignment | 12,508 |

The 13 priced candidates without consistent official area assignment remain in the view. A further 96 priced candidates have no reachable Phase 6 station; station minutes remain NULL, not a fabricated or median route. The common model-comparison set omits those 109 from *all* ladder steps to hold evaluation rows fixed. An opportunity-only sensitivity can retain the 96 disconnected cases by omitting station time from every compared model. All 23,144 rows remain available for price-observation diagnosis.

Bathroom QA revealed that a missing structured bathroom value is usually available in the source `bathrooms_text`: 1,674/12,521 primary candidates use a transparent numeric/`Half-bath` source-text fallback. This is deterministic source recovery, **not statistical imputation**. No primary candidate lacks `bathrooms_effective`. In one candidate, structured `1.5` disagrees with text `1 bath`; the structured value takes precedence, and `bathrooms_source_disagreement` flags it. The runner fails if the present source no longer supports this coverage or if the district code/name mapping changes.

## Context legitimacy

The ten numbered official City districts align with City StatBank district codes `1001`–`1010` and names after one punctuation normalisation (`Vesterbro/Kongens Enghave` versus `Vesterbro-Kongens Enghave`). **The official WFS boundary vintage still has no verified reference date**, so this is a code/name crosswalk, not proof of identical historical polygons. The view marks 20,567 listings (11,203 primary candidates) `district_code_name_match_vintage_unverified`. Frederiksberg is a single municipality-sized proxy, marked for 2,499 listings (1,305 primary candidates); its national context is in separate columns. The 78 unassigned listings (13 primary candidates) have no contextual area value. No City district income/population is copied into Frederiksberg fields or pooled with its nonharmonised municipality measures. The 2026-Q3 population period postdates some Airbnb scrapes; 2024 income has a different vintage. Consequently, municipality category and City Hall distance are the only primary geographic controls; context is descriptive or guarded Copenhagen-only sensitivity, and no district population density is claimed.

## Missing-data diagnosis

The reproducible [missingness report](../outputs/tables/phase07/missingness_report.csv) lists each variable by all listings, pre-price residential population and priced primary candidates, further broken down by official municipality, official validation area, property type, and scrape date. Predictor missingness among priced candidates is also tabulated by **observed price quartile** (diagnostic only). No quartile is assigned where price is missing. The [priced-versus-missing comparison](../outputs/tables/phase07/price_missingness_comparison.csv) shows, among 21,080 conventional entire homes, 12,521 observed prices and **8,559 missing/invalid (40.6%)**. Both groups have median accommodates 3, bedrooms 1, bathrooms 1; observed-price listings have median minimum nights 2 versus 3 for missing-price listings. These summaries do not establish missing-at-random.

Two complementary maps are generated from PostGIS in the **Phase 4 cartographic style**: the same wide frame, subdued neighbouring municipalities, official outlines, title/subtitle typography, right-hand key, north arrow, metric scale bar and source line. The [500 m square-grid map](../outputs/figures/phase07/missing_price_grid.png) colours EPSG:25832 cells by the **count** of conventional entire-home listings with no valid listed price. Squares with fewer than five missing-price listings are omitted for disclosure control: 236 published cells show **8,346/8,559** missing prices; 213 are in suppressed sparse cells. All eligible listing points, including the 61 without an official area assignment, contribute to the grid aggregation. A high grid count can reflect a high listing concentration rather than a higher probability of price missingness. The separate [official-area map](../outputs/figures/phase07/missing_price_area.png) restores the **percentage** of conventional entire-home listings missing price among the 21,019 listings assigned to one of the 10 Copenhagen districts or Frederiksberg municipality. The 61 without an official area are excluded only from this percentage map, not from the grid or diagnostic tables. Area rates range from **33.19% in `cph_01`** to **44.40% in `cph_03`**. Both figures contain aggregates only, no listing points or row-level coordinates.

Among the 12,521 priced candidates: bedrooms 124 NULL (0.99%); raw beds 1,291 NULL (10.31%); structured bathrooms 1,674 NULL (13.37%) but effective bathrooms 0 NULL; `instant_bookable` 12,521 NULL (100%); ratings 1,911 NULL (15.26%); official municipality/area 13 NULL (0.10%); walking station time 96 NULL (0.77%). All eight Phase-2-screened amenity indicators, accommodates, minimum nights, superhost and host listing count are observed. District-only context is naturally NULL for Frederiksberg and unassigned rows; Frederiksberg-only context is naturally NULL for Copenhagen. These **structural geography NULLs are not predictor missingness to impute**. No MICE is warranted for the main model after source-text bathroom recovery and exclusion of entirely unavailable instant booking; any rating-inclusive explanatory sensitivity will need an explicit complete-case/MICE comparison. XGBoost native missingness is optional only for predictor sensitivities. No outcome imputation is allowed.

## Redundancy and spatial validation

[Pairwise diagnostics](../outputs/tables/phase07/redundancy_pairs.csv) use primary candidate rows, with pairwise complete observations and no outcome-based selection. Spearman correlations are 0.806 for accommodates/bedrooms, 0.810 for accommodates/beds, 0.952 for Euclidean/walking food counts at the primary threshold, 0.914 for cultural counts, and 0.759 for Euclidean station distance/walking station time. The strongest amenity-pair Phi correlation is only 0.282 (dishwasher/private balcony); all eight remain fixed from Phase 2. [VIFs](../outputs/tables/phase07/vif_diagnostics.csv) are a *diagnostic deliberately including Euclidean and walking measures together* on 11,038 complete cases: food Euclidean 28.19, food walking 22.04, cultural Euclidean 19.63, cultural walking 18.38. This supports separate E and W main specifications rather than a combined coefficient interpretation. At the distinct-area level, district population/households have Spearman 0.952 across **10** City districts ([area diagnostics](../outputs/tables/phase07/context_area_redundancy.csv)); repeating these values at listing level would overstate independent contextual evidence. No variable was selected by p-value or price performance.

Five location-only outer folds were persisted in `analysis.spatial_cv_folds_v1`: 1-km EPSG:25832 blocks, 111 unique blocks, `StratifiedGroupKFold` by municipality, seed 20260929, all 23,066 officially assigned listings. Every block belongs to exactly one fold; every fold contains both municipalities. [Aggregate fold counts](../outputs/tables/phase07/spatial_cv_folds.csv) are reproducible. The priced-candidate fold sizes are **3,947 / 1,628 / 2,768 / 1,674 / 2,491**; imbalance is recorded rather than tuned on outcomes. The fixed common-set sizes are 3,923 / 1,621 / 2,733 / 1,656 / 2,479. Existing assignments are compared on rerun and never silently overwritten. Adjacent 1-km blocks can still share spatial context; this CV is not a geographic buffer split.

The full [pre-analysis specification](../docs/preanalysis_specification.md) freezes population, outcome, primary controls, missing-data handling, E/W accessibility ladder, thresholds, folds/seeds, metrics and robustness. This phase performs diagnostic correlations/VIFs and selection summaries only—**no final price EDA, OLS, Elastic Net, XGBoost or model tuning**. Outstanding limitations are price observation selection, official polygon/statistical-vintage crosswalk, 13 area gaps, 96 disconnected station routes, September OSM versus June–July listing timing, user-confirmed rather than provider-declared DKK, and lack of GTFS approval.
