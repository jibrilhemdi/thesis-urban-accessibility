# Phase 9 — primary price models

Run completed 2026-09-29. This report implements the user-requested Phase 9 against the [Phase 7 freeze](../docs/preanalysis_specification.md), the [Phase 8 saved folds](phase08_eda_and_cv.md), and the pre-fit deviations in the [decision log](../docs/decision_log.md). The outcome is `ln(price_nightly)`: a *listed* nightly DKK price, not a transaction or occupancy measure. DKK is user-confirmed, not encoded explicitly in the source price string. No outcome was imputed and no raw file was modified.

## Design and reproducibility

The authoritative input is `analysis.analysis_dataset_v1`, joined on `(snapshot_date, listing_id)` to `analysis.cv_assignments` and the Phase 9 bus robustness table. The same **12,412** positive-price, official-area, reachable-walking-station listings enter every primary comparison. The 13 unassigned and 96 unreachable-station Phase 2 candidates remain excluded from this *common comparison*, not deleted from the database. Among these 12,412, `bedrooms` is missing for 123 (0.99%); every other selected control is observed. The prepared full analytical view retains missing-price listings for the Phase 7 selection diagnostics; they do not enter model fitting. Source scrape date is provenance, not a price-replacement rule.

M0 comprises the frozen property/host block (six-category property type, accommodates, bedrooms, source-supported effective bathrooms, log1p minimum nights, superhost, log1p host listing count, eight preselected amenities), plus official municipality and projected City Hall distance. In a pooled model, the heterogeneous City district and Frederiksberg municipality income/population series cannot legitimately be treated as equivalent neighbourhood measurements, so they are excluded from M0. ME adds nearest rail/metro station straight-line km and log1p food/social and cultural/tourist counts within 800 m. MW replaces those terms with nearest station walking minutes and the two log1p 10-minute walking counts. MEW includes both E and W blocks, **exploratory** because their corresponding measures are highly correlated. The canonical POI/station sets and thresholds were fixed before fitting.

Outer evaluation uses *exactly* the saved Phase 8 five random folds and 11 leave-one-official-area-out folds; all four feature sets use identical held-out listings. The frozen Phase 7 one-kilometre grid assignments remain unchanged in PostGIS but were **not run as an outer scheme in this user-directed Phase 9**. The no-feature benchmark predicts the training-fold mean log price. OLS uses training-fold median plus a missingness indicator and training-fold one-hot encoding. XGBoost uses the same training-only preprocessing, with three predeclared regularised configurations selected by four **grouped** inner folds within each outer training set; no outer-fold outcome enters preprocessing or tuning. Inner groups are saved Phase 7 1-km blocks, seed 20260930, stratified by municipality. Outer random seed is 20260929. No MICE is warranted for the 0.99% bedroom gap under the frozen rule; a separate complete-case explanatory OLS sensitivity is reported. The database NULLs are not overwritten. XGBoost's native missing-value facility is not used in this primary comparison. Elastic Net, present in the earlier freeze, is deferred under the user's Phase 9 model-family restriction rather than silently added.

`outputs/tables/phase09_run_metadata.json` records the specification SHA-256, exact feature names, three XGBoost candidate configurations, versions, seeds and sample rules. Software: NumPy 2.1.3, pandas 2.2.3, SciPy 1.15.3, scikit-learn 1.6.1, statsmodels 0.14.4, XGBoost 3.0.5. The versioned, hash-checked archived OSM extract is described in the [Phase 5 report](phase05_euclidean_accessibility.md). The OSM extract is 2026-09-26, about three months later than the Airbnb snapshot: accessibility describes that later map, not verified contemporaneous June destinations.

### Destination-boundary audit of the current fitted models

The Phase 5/6 canonical destination set is buffered **beyond** Copenhagen and Frederiksberg, as frozen to avoid an artificial accessibility drop at the study edge. The current model therefore can use external destinations; the Phase 8 maps correctly display nearby external POIs/stations rather than implying that all model destinations lie within the two municipalities. The reproducible, aggregate-only `make phase9-boundary-audit` reads PostGIS and writes `outputs/tables/phase09_destination_boundary_audit.csv`. It uses `ST_Covers(spatial.study_area.geom_25832, destination.geom_25832)` and finds 691 of 3,006 food/social POIs, 191 of 463 cultural/tourist POIs, and 78 of 143 stations outside the official two-municipality union. For the 12,521 primary candidates, external POIs enter the 800 m Euclidean food measure for 1,476 listings and the cultural measure for 1,165; 74 listings have an external Euclidean-nearest station and 80 an external walking-nearest station. These counts describe affected listings, not a proportion of each listing's opportunities. Among candidates with an official area, 2,243 have a walking-nearest station **inside** the two municipalities but across their own official CV-area border. Neither Euclidean search nor network routing stops at district/municipality boundaries: straight-line station distance uses EPSG:25832 `ST_Distance`, while walking time uses the shortest permitted pedestrian path plus listing/station snap connectors at 80 m/min. This rule permits a border listing's closest station to be in a neighbouring district.

Restricting *destination points* to the municipal union would change the frozen measure and potentially create border artefacts; it would not by itself constrain walking paths to stay inside the union. The displayed models and metrics in this report **have not been silently redefined**. A municipality-only destination definition requires an explicit new version/sensitivity or authorised replacement of the frozen primary specification, with re-computation of matched Euclidean and walking features and model outputs.

## Explanatory semilog OLS

Full-sample OLS uses HC3 heteroskedasticity-robust standard errors. In MW, one additional station walking minute is associated with a coefficient of **-0.00154** (95% CI -0.00278 to -0.00029), approximately -0.15% in listed price conditional on the included controls. The food/social `log1p` count coefficient is -0.0207 (CI -0.0307 to -0.0107); the cultural/tourist coefficient is +0.0705 (CI +0.0617 to +0.0793). Because the latter regressors are `ln(1+count)`, a unit change is *not* one extra POI; it corresponds to multiplying `1+count` by e. ME's nearest-station km coefficient is -0.0557 (CI -0.0773 to -0.0341); its food/social and cultural/tourist coefficients are -0.0275 and +0.0822. These are conditional associations, not treatment effects, and may reflect neighbourhood selection or omitted spatial factors. The positive cultural/tourist association and negative conditional food/social association should not be read as causal amenity effects.

In-sample R² is 0.4580/0.4753/0.4708/0.4753 for M0/ME/MW/MEW; use the out-of-fold table for predictive comparison. MEW's condition number is 197 (versus 107 for ME), and its walking coefficients lose stable independent interpretation once the highly correlated E terms are included. Complete-case OLS excludes only 123 missing-bedroom listings (N=12,289), with similar ME/MW accessibility coefficients and intervals. HC3 handles heteroskedasticity, **not** residual spatial dependence; these intervals may still overstate precision for spatially clustered data.

## Spatial residuals and model interpretation

Residual Moran's I uses **outer out-of-fold** observed-minus-predicted log prices, directed eight-nearest-neighbour weights, row-standardisation and EPSG:25832 coordinates. Each diagnostic uses 499 two-sided permutations (minimum attainable p=0.002):

| Scheme | OLS M0 | OLS MW | XGBoost M0 | XGBoost MW |
|:--|--:|--:|--:|--:|
| Random | 0.0987 | 0.0783 | 0.0880 | 0.0391 |
| Geographic | 0.1387 | 0.1173 | 0.1569 | 0.1386 |

Every permutation p-value is 0.002. MW reduces, but does not remove, residual clustering. Geographic OOF residuals are mapped both as **official held-out-area means** and as **500 m square-grid means**, with one zero-centred diverging colour scale across all four OLS/XGBoost MW maps. The area views show broad regional bias; the grid reveals within-area patterns that an 11-area choropleth hides. It is a display aggregation of the *same held-out-area predictions*, **not** a grid-CV result or a separately fitted model. Grid cells require at least 10 contributing listings, are clipped to the official study footprint in EPSG:25832, and otherwise remain pale blue. The 211 displayed cells represent 11,804 of 12,412 model listings; 608 are in omitted sparse cells, as recorded in `phase09_geographic_residual_grid_coverage.csv`. The five-listing Phase 8 price-map rule is tightened here because prediction residuals can be more sensitive and unstable in small cells. On the shared colour scale, area means look necessarily subtler than local grid extremes. No listing point, row-level prediction, or cell-level data table is public. For the XGBoost MW *full-sample descriptive* model, `phase09_xgb_mw_shap.csv` aggregates TreeSHAP absolute contributions. This is feature attribution for a fitted model, **not** causal importance, out-of-fold evidence or a reason to revise the frozen set. Correlated features can redistribute contributions.

## Separate bus-access robustness

The pre-Phase 9 amendment is implemented with migration `014` and `src/pipeline/run_bus_proximity_phase9.py`. It reuses and verifies the SHA-256 of the immutable Phase 5 PBF; it does **not** request another OSM snapshot. The documented selection is `highway=bus_stop` or `public_transport=platform` with `bus=yes`, for nodes and ways. It stores 4,351 source objects in `spatial.bus_stops` and projected EPSG:25832 nearest-point distance for all 23,144 listings in `features.bus_stop_proximity`; no feature distance exceeds the source-edge clearance. It is a simple OSM bus-stop proxy, not a deduplicated service/route inventory, walking route, frequency, waiting time or reachable-destination measure. The OSM source postdates the price snapshot.

Adding this distance to MW as **MW_BUS_secondary** changes pooled geographic RMSE by only -0.00042 for OLS and -0.00069 for XGBoost (negative change means lower error); random improvements are similarly below 0.0006. Full-sample HC3 OLS gives a positive association for being farther from a stop, +0.228 log-price units per km (CI +0.158 to +0.298). This potentially counterintuitive coefficient is susceptible to spatial confounding and should not be interpreted as an effect of bus service. Bus proximity is excluded from M0/ME/MW/MEW. GTFS remains unavailable pending approval; no schedule-sensitive transit result is claimed.
