The mapping matches all ten `spatial.official_copenhagen_districts` names after only district 1004's `/` versus `-` punctuation normalization. Every district appears once in the [source audit table](../outputs/tables/context_district_source_audit.csv). The selected City extracts acquired **29 September 2026** represent **2026 Q3** population and **2024** income; their 22 September archived counterparts contain byte-identical `KKBEF1`/`KKIND3` CSVs. `KKHUS1` differs between downloads because the later archive deliberately selects **2026 Q1** rather than Q3. `KKBOL3` selects 2026. Acquisition date is not a statistical period.

National `FOLK1A` selects municipality 101/147, all sex/age/marital statuses, 2026 Q3 (population at the start of the quarter). National `INDKP106` selects average income for persons in the total group (`ENHED=118`, `KOEN=MOK`, `ALDER1=00`, `INDKINTB=000`), 2024. The 22 September Frederiksberg-only archive corroborates the current two-municipality extract: **105,947** people and **344,810 DKK** mean disposable income. Frederiksberg is **one municipality-level proxy**, not a neighbourhood or an eleventh official Copenhagen district. National `FAM55N`, `BOL101`, `BOL106` remain separate household/dwelling series; none is substituted for population or added to the new context model. Source paths and IDs are retained in [the 11-area export](../outputs/tables/analysis_area_context.csv) and `meta.source_files`; the runner verifies selected input file SHA-256 values against registered hashes.

## Population reconciliation and polygon area

The ten City `KKBEF1` district counts sum to **666,861**. The national `FOLK1A` Copenhagen municipality total for 2026 Q3 is **670,389**: an absolute difference of **3,528**, or **0.526%** of the national municipality total. Both are all-age total population and share a period, but provider methodology/geographic frames are not proven identical, so the district values were **not rescaled**. Frederiksberg contributes 105,947 on its own municipality basis.

Area is `ST_Area` of the *actual assignment polygons* in **EPSG:25832**, divided by 1,000,000 for km²; source boundary GeoJSON was EPSG:4326. No degree-based area calculation or land-only correction was used. The ten City district polygon areas sum to **92.829469 km²**, while the separate official Copenhagen municipality polygon is **91.697949 km²**. District polygons do not overlap one another, but their union extends **1.383645 km² outside** the municipal polygon and leaves **0.252126 km² of it uncovered**. Their net area excess is **1.131520 km²**. This demonstrates a real boundary-product/vintage mismatch, not a rounding error. The City WFS district vintage and treatment of harbour/water are undocumented in the archive; these are **polygon/gross-area densities**, not verified land-only densities or exact census-area density. The Frederiksberg assignment polygon is **8.708695 km²**, sourced from the national municipality layer. These caveats are material to any area-context model.

`population_density_per_km2 = population_count / area_km2`; `log_population_density = ln(density)`. All 11 populations, areas and densities are positive and finite. Across the 11 mixed-resolution areas, population spans **40,883–105,947** (median 65,969), polygon area **4.108–19.365 km²** (median 8.741), density **4,720–19,376 persons/km²** (median 7,143), and 2024 income **248,618–389,913 DKK/person** (median 300,408). Frederiksberg's density is **12,166 persons/km²**; the comparison to City districts is descriptive and subject to the above boundary/source caveats.

## Income-definition audit

| Attribute | City `KKIND3` | National `INDKP106` (Frederiksberg) |
|---|---|---|
| Concept | Mean disposable income for people **with the selected income type** | Mean personal disposable income for persons in selected total income group |
| Population/age | City table title says persons aged 14+; selected sex total | Provider description says persons aged 14+; selected `ALDER1=00` total, both sexes |
| Unit | DKK per person with income type, `var2=003` | DKK per person in group, `ENHED=118` |
| Period | 2024 | 2024 |
| Geography/producer | Copenhagen districts / City Statbank | Frederiksberg municipality / Statistics Denmark StatBank |

The published selections agree on broad concept, unit and year, **not on a proven identical denominator or processing definition**. `analysis_area_income_definition_differs=true` is retained for the Frederiksberg row. The income field is not described as fully harmonised. Likewise the household and dwelling comparability flags remain true for **all** rows because those definitions were not validated by this audit.

As a **plausibility diagnostic only**, weighting the ten City district mean incomes by their **2026 Q3 total population** yields **300,800 DKK/person** (unrounded 300,799.72), compared with national Copenhagen `INDKP106` **295,836 DKK/person** in 2024: **+4,964 DKK, +1.678%** relative to the national value. The 2026 total-population weights are *not* the 2024 income-eligible denominator; the difference cannot establish or refute definitional equivalence.

## Scientific disposition and next model step

**All ten Copenhagen districts are represented correctly by code/name; Frederiksberg is correctly represented at municipality scale.** The two population products are close in total but not identical, and polygon differences further limit area-density comparability. The 11-area mixed-resolution context can be **scientifically defensible only as a clearly labelled exploratory/sensitivity association**, not as eleven harmonised neighbourhoods or an update to Phase 9 M0. The income concept is especially uncertain across providers; neither the pooled income coefficient nor its nominal listing-level standard error should be presented as a neighbourhood causal effect. There are only 11 independent contextual units, so listing-level N does not supply 12,412 independent income/density observations. The Frederiksberg proxy, boundary vintage/water, temporal mismatch (2024 income; Q3 2026 population), and 78 unassigned listings must remain visible.

For a subsequent, separately registered **Copenhagen-only** exploratory association, the eligible context fields are `log_analysis_area_population_density` (provisional polygon density) and `analysis_area_income_100k_dkk`, with `analysis_area_code`, source/period and boundary caveats retained. For a separately labelled **11-area** mixed-resolution sensitivity, `log_analysis_area_population_density` may be examined with `analysis_area_is_municipality_proxy`/municipality classification disclosed; the pooled income field should be at most a guarded diagnostic, not a fully harmonised control. Use a consistent sample and account for area-level dependence; do not add area fixed effects alongside area-constant context or claim listing-level independent context variation. Keep household/dwelling fields, `FAM55N`, `BOL101`, `BOL106`, and the 2026 Q3 `KKHUS1` archive **out** of the next model step. No model has been run here.

Remaining verification before a strong pooled contextual claim: statistical-vintage correspondence of City WFS districts, an authoritative land-area/water definition, comparable 2024 income denominator documentation, and ideally Frederiksberg submunicipal population/income geography. This audit stops before price-model estimation.
