# Phase 3 — contextual demographic and housing data

Date: 2026-09-29. This phase audited and ingested the **existing archive**; it did not download new statistics, calculate density, join context to listings, or fit models. The authoritative derived layer is PostgreSQL/PostGIS. Run `make phase3-run` to reproduce the raw imports, clean measures and [source assessment](../outputs/tables/context_source_assessment.csv); run `THESIS_PHASE3_TEST=1 make phase3-test` for live QA.

## Ingestion and resolution

Eighteen immutable CSV extracts were loaded into separate all-text `raw` tables: five historical Frederiksberg-only national extracts (2026-09-22), five Copenhagen-plus-Frederiksberg national extracts (2026-09-29), and four City of Copenhagen district extracts at each of two retrieval dates (2026-09-22 and 2026-09-29). Every import has a source-file SHA-256, source URL where recorded, row count and import log in `meta`; the 18 source versions and their definitions are listed individually in `context_source_assessment.csv`. Historical files remain in `raw`, not silently overwritten. The earlier City `KKHUS1` uses 2026 Q3; the selected newer extract uses **2026 Q1 / 1 January**. The older Frederiksberg-only tidy context was previously superseded and was not imported as an analytical source.

Two distinct, long-form clean tables were built: `clean.municipality_context_measures` (12 rows = six measures × two municipalities) and `clean.copenhagen_district_context_measures` (70 rows = seven measures × ten City districts). Every row has its own geography key, reference period, unit, concept definition and raw source-file ID. No municipality totals were copied onto listings or into district columns. The tables are deliberately separate because their geographic resolutions, producers and some denominators differ.

| Source | Concept and unit | Reference period | Extract geography | Population/denominator definition |

| National `FOLK1A` | Total population, persons | 2026 Q3 (first day) | Copenhagen 101; Frederiksberg 147 | All ages, sexes and marital statuses |

This follows both the archived selections and provider definitions: [Statistics Denmark household table](https://www.dst.dk/en/Statistik/udgivelser/nyt/relateret?pid=567), [housing census definition](https://www.dst.dk/en/Statistik/dokumentation/documentationofstatistics/census-of-housing/statistical-presentation), and [personal-income table listing](https://www.dst.dk/en/Statistik/udgivelser/nyt/relateret?pid=292). The assessment CSV records each archived version's geography, date, unit, population definition, submunicipal variation and intended use.

## Which variables may enter a listing-level model?

1. **Suitable primary listing-level controls:** a Copenhagen-versus-Frederiksberg `municipality_i` indicator can be derived from the listing's provider neighbourhood label (20,644 Copenhagen-labelled and 2,500 Frederiksberg-labelled listings; 11,216 and 1,305 primary-sample candidates respectively). It is a *location category*, not a national population or income value. Its boundary assignment remains provisional until official polygon validation. It is redundant with exhaustive 11-area fixed effects, so model specifications must not include both. No national statistical measure here is a listing-level characteristic.
2. **Municipality-level descriptive/context variables:** the two national rows can describe the municipalities separately. The selected totals are population 670,389 / 105,947; households 332,181 / 55,393; dwelling stock 355,352 / 57,781; dwellings with registered population 331,766 / 55,371 (Copenhagen / Frederiksberg). National average disposable incomes are 295,836 / 344,810 DKK in **2024**, not individual listing or host income. Repeating either value over listing rows does not create 23,144 independent contextual observations.
3. **Robustness-only context:** the ten City district counts and average income can be explored in a **Copenhagen-only** district-level contextual specification with explicit area clustering/uncertainty and vintage caveats; they are area attributes, not individual income. A pooled 10-district-plus-one-Frederiksberg-proxy specification is exploratory mixed-resolution sensitivity only, with geographic/source flags and without claiming harmonised income or housing definitions. The selected City population total is 666,861 versus national Copenhagen's 670,389 (difference 3,528); households are 332,166 versus 332,181 (difference 15). Equal household reference dates do not establish equal methods or boundaries. National income is effectively a two-municipality contrast and is collinear with a municipality indicator in a pooled listing regression.
4. **Unusable for the proposed primary contextual controls:** national `FOLK1A`, `INDKP106`, `FAM55N`, `BOL101` and `BOL106` as *neighbourhood* variables; `BOL106` as a population count; `BOL101` occupied-dwelling counts as resident counts; pooled City/national income as a harmonised 11-area measure. The historical Frederiksberg-only tidy row must not be used to manufacture an 11th comparable City district.
5. **Missing and requiring acquisition/validation:** harmonised submunicipal Frederiksberg population and income (and, if used, housing) with verified small-area boundaries and a listing-to-area crosswalk; official Copenhagen district polygons/boundary definitions and appropriate land-area measurements; an official Frederiksberg municipality boundary or compatible small-area polygons; and income/housing documentation sufficient to harmonise sources if a pooled area-income variable is essential. The archived Inside Airbnb GeoJSON has 11 provider `MultiPolygon` features, including one Frederiksberg area, but no explicit CRS member or independently validated official boundaries/land-area denominator. It is **not yet a defensible density denominator**. No area or density was computed in this phase; later spatial calculation must record CRS (metric EPSG:25832, after source CRS verification) and treatment of water/boundary slivers.

Consequently, `log(population_density_i)` is **not available** at listing/neighbourhood level because validated areas are missing, and it cannot be produced for Frederiksberg neighbourhoods from a single municipality total. `income_i` is available only as a Copenhagen district contextual proxy in a restricted sensitivity, not as individual or consistently defined pooled neighbourhood income. `municipality_i` is conditionally feasible from listing location labels; it is not a demographic measure. The primary price-model ladder should not claim neighbourhood population density or income until the missing source and polygon checks are resolved. No predictor was filled, no outcome was imputed, and no Phase 4 accessibility or modelling work was performed.

## Validation and outstanding issues

All four Phase 3 tests passed against PostgreSQL. The selected national tables reproduced the previously audited municipality totals, and the City district population/household sums reproduced their previously audited totals. Source-cell parsing rejects missing, negative, nonfinite and noninteger count values rather than filling them. All selected clean measures have source-file lineage; Frederiksberg is absent from `clean.copenhagen_district_context_measures`. Raw values are preserved as text. The main outstanding issues are official area geography/land area, Frederiksberg submunicipal statistics, definition harmonisation, and the 2024 income lag relative to June–July 2026 Airbnb listings.
