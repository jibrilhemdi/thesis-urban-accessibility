The configured authoritative endpoint is the loopback-bound Docker thesis database `thesis_accessibility` on `127.0.0.1:5433`, PostgreSQL **16.9** and PostGIS **3.5.2**, with **17** registered checksum-verified migrations and **58** registered source files. Its 11 analysis areas are ten official Copenhagen city districts plus Frederiksberg. The authoritative `analysis.analysis_dataset_v1` query returns **12,412 unique** common comparison listings, all with positive observed listed nightly price, valid `ln(price_nightly)`, official area and reachable walking station. ## Frozen interpretation and edit policy

RQ1: Euclidean and network measures are empirically different. RQ2: access modestly improves the frozen base, but stable incremental prediction beyond observed area socioeconomic context is **not supported**, nor is consistent walking superiority over Euclidean access. RQ3: geographic/block/buffered validation differs from random and exposes weaker transfer, but the gaps cannot all be attributed to leakage alone. All are non-causal claims about observed listed prices. [Final claim matrix](../outputs/tables/conclusion_stability.csv); [RQ-organised synthesis](final_scientific_synthesis.md).
