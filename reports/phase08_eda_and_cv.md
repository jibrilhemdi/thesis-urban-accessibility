## Spatial autocorrelation and candidate separation scales

[Moran diagnostics](../outputs/tables/phase08_spatial_moran.csv) use **directed 8-nearest-neighbour** weights on projected listing points, self excluded, each row standardised to sum to 1 (`S0=N`); there are 100,168 directed edges for the 12,521-observation complete variables. For station time, 12,425 non-NULL candidates define a separate 99,400-edge graph. Distances and neighbour search use **EPSG:25832 metres**. A fixed-seed, two-sided 499-permutation reference gives p-value resolution 0.002, not exact zero. Duplicate/anonymised point positions can make the identity of tied nearest neighbours arbitrary; the deterministic row order and SciPy query define the reproduced choice. These are spatial *marginal-variable* diagnostics, not model-residual tests.

| Variable | N | Moran's I | Permutation p |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Log listed price | 12,521 | 0.2465 | 0.002 |
| Food/social, Euclidean 800 m | 12,521 | 0.9991 | 0.002 |
| Food/social, walking 10 min | 12,521 | 0.9885 | 0.002 |
| Cultural/tourist, Euclidean 800 m | 12,521 | 0.9991 | 0.002 |
| Cultural/tourist, walking 10 min | 12,521 | 0.9867 | 0.002 |
| Nearest station, walking minutes | 12,425 | 0.9593 | 0.002 |

Opportunity counts are spatially smoothed by construction, so their near-one Moran values should **not** be interpreted as independent evidence about Airbnb demand. To gauge separation distance, the [sampled distance-bin diagnostics](../outputs/tables/phase08_distance_bin_covariance.csv) draw 600,000 random ordered listing pairs per variable (self-pairs removed) and report mean standardised cross-products in 0–250, 250–500, 500–1,000, 1,000–2,000, 2,000–4,000 and 4,000–8,000 m bins. For log price these are **0.165, 0.152, 0.122, 0.056, −0.031, −0.011**. This is a descriptive sampled covariance curve, **not** a formal variogram or model residual correlation. Its decline by roughly 1–2 km, combined with the 800 m primary walking budget and the existing 1-km block size, motivates **500 m and 1,000 m** buffered leave-area-out training exclusions. A 2-km candidate was inspected using geography/sample counts only but not frozen: it would remove 7,738 priced training candidates around `cph_01` alone, leaving a small and differently supported training set. No model performance was consulted.

## Persisted outer-fold schemes

Migration `013_phase8_cv_assignments.sql` creates `analysis.cv_assignments` (one row for **every 23,144** listing, preserving unpriced rows) and `analysis.cv_buffer_exclusions` (sparse, held-out-area-specific training exclusions). Identifiers are built without price or accessibility values and are compared exactly on rerun; neither table is silently overwritten. Seeds and CRS/version are stored per row. The original **five 1-km spatial folds in `analysis.spatial_cv_folds_v1` are unchanged** and copied into the new assignment table as the pre-analysis primary outer split. All comparable Phase 9+ models must read persisted assignments, use the same 12,412-row common sample for the main ladder, and fit preprocessing/tuning only on each training portion.

|---|---|---|

| Buffered leave-area-out | Prepared, **not yet modelled**; 500 m and 1,000 m EPSG:25832 polygon-to-train-point exclusions | Same LOAO test folds; training N differs by area/buffer |

The 1.5-km seed was chosen from a **geography-only** ordered search for five folds that each had at least 100 Frederiksberg source listings and a largest/smallest full-fold ratio below 3; no outcome or model fit was examined. Whole grid cells stay in one fold. For random CV, all source listings were shuffled before price eligibility, so missing price does not influence assignment. For LOAO, every area's original primary N is at least 221; no area is merged solely for fold size. District polygons have unverified historical vintage relative to the City statistical extracts, but are official geography for this CV. Frederiksberg is never described as a Copenhagen district. [Fold summaries](../outputs/tables/phase08_cv_fold_summary.csv) report N, price quartiles/range, log-price SD, accessibility medians and major ranges for every outer fold.

The [per-area training/test support table](../outputs/tables/phase08_geographic_support.csv) reports min/P5/median/P95/max and percentage of held-out values outside the training min–max for each major numeric control/accessibility measure, plus unseen municipality and property-type categories. Key extrapolation risks: holding out `cph_01` puts **30.66%** of its walk-food counts above the training range (32.24% for Euclidean food); holding out `cph_07` puts **24.89%** of its centre distances outside the training range; holding out Frederiksberg leaves its municipality category **100% unseen** in training. This last fold cannot estimate a Frederiksberg-specific category effect from its own training split; it must be labelled as structural geographic extrapolation, not silently treated as ordinary interpolation. All common-sample LOAO test folds have at least 221 observations; no standalone fold was merged.

The [buffer-feasibility table](../outputs/tables/phase08_buffer_feasibility.csv) lists test N, original training N, additional training exclusions and retained N for each of the 11 areas at 500 and 1,000 m. Exclusions are computed with `ST_DWithin` from **other-area** EPSG:25832 listing points to the held-out official polygon; held-out rows are test observations and are never inserted as buffer exclusions. The 500 m exclusions are nested within 1,000 m. At 1,000 m, the largest proportional training loss is **44.63%** when Frederiksberg is held out (6,151 common candidates remain), followed by **38.20%** for `cph_01` (6,538 remain). These are candidate robustness designs, not completed model results.

## Boundaries and limitations

Both the original 1-km and new 1.5-km grouped splits can put neighbouring blocks on opposite sides of a train/test boundary; only the buffered LOAO preparation enforces a geometric gap between a held-out polygon and retained training points. Random CV is expected to be optimistic under spatial dependence. Administrative LOAO has unequal N and may extrapolate to unseen locations/categories. The original Airbnb points may be anonymised, so 500/1,000 m near-boundary exclusions and kNN weights describe the available point proxies, not exact addresses. The spatial-weight/permutation diagnostics use observed listed prices, but **no outer fold was used to select a model, POI taxonomy, threshold or hyperparameter**. Phase 9 may compute residual spatial diagnostics after models are fitted; Phase 8 intentionally stops before that work.
