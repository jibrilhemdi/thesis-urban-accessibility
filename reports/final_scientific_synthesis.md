# Final scientific synthesis for thesis writing

The empirical case is the **Copenhagen–Frederiksberg urban core**: Copenhagen Municipality plus the administratively separate Frederiksberg Municipality. The common price-model sample comprises 12,412 conventional entire-home listings with an observed positive listed nightly price, official-area assignment and reachable walking station. Price is treated as DKK by user confirmation, is not imputed, and is modelled as its natural logarithm. The authoritative listing and feature data remain in PostgreSQL/PostGIS; the cited CSVs are frozen machine-readable results. This synthesis is predictive/associational, not causal. [Primary report](phase09_main_models.md); [pre-freeze resolution](pre_freeze_resolution.md).

## RQ1 — What does network measurement change?

**Question.** Do straight-line and pedestrian-network measures describe the same access to a fixed destination set?

**Core evidence.** The Phase 5 `phase05_v2` universe contains 3,006 food/social POIs, 463 cultural/tourist POIs and 143 qualifying rail/metro/S-train stations. Euclidean and walking measures use these **same deduplicated destinations**. The primary pair compares an 800 m straight-line radius with a 10-minute walk (800 m route budget at 4.8 km/h, including snap connectors). Memorial-only objects and bus/tram-only stops are excluded from the respective primary groups. [Canonical inventory](../outputs/tables/final/appendix_destination_counts.csv); [taxonomy](../outputs/tables/final/appendix_destination_taxonomy.csv); [destination audit](appendix_destination_inventory.md).

**Result and robustness.** Matched 800 m/10-minute counts differ for 99.4% of common-sample listings for food/social destinations and 82.0% for cultural/tourist destinations, although the measures are strongly correlated. Differences persist at matched 1,200 m/15-minute and 1,600 m/20-minute sensitivity thresholds. The primary buffered destination universe allows reachable endpoints just outside the two municipalities; an endpoint-only union restriction changes some edge listings' measures but does not materially alter the modelling conclusion. Routes and district crossings are never clipped at administrative lines. [Representation table](../outputs/tables/phase10/accessibility_representation.csv); [threshold performance](../outputs/tables/phase10/model_performance.csv); [boundary audit](pre_freeze_boundary_sensitivity.md).

**Conclusion and limit.** Euclidean and walking-network accessibility are empirically different representations of nearby opportunities. This measurement finding alone does **not** establish that network measures predict listed price better. Airbnb coordinates are approximate, mapped destinations may be incomplete, and the September 2026 OSM snapshot postdates the June/July Airbnb scrape.

## RQ2 — Does accessibility improve price prediction beyond other controls?

**Question.** What does accessibility add beyond property/host/location variables, and does that increment survive observed area socioeconomic context?

**Base specification.** Frozen Phase 9 M0 uses property/listing and host/rental controls, municipality, and City Hall centrality. ME adds Euclidean access; MW adds walking-network access; MEW combines both but is exploratory because of collinearity. All compared models use the same 12,412 listings and saved folds. Under primary leave-one-area-out (LOAO) XGBoost validation, M0/ME/MW RMSE of log listed price is 0.33277/0.32795/0.32976. Thus ME and MW lower held-out error by about 0.00482 and 0.00301 respectively; MW does **not** consistently outperform ME. OLS gives the same modest-gain ordering. These are associations/predictions, not amenity effects. [Phase 9 performance](../outputs/tables/phase09/model_performance.csv); [incremental table](../outputs/tables/phase09/incremental_accessibility.csv); [OLS coefficients](../outputs/tables/phase09/ols_coefficients.csv).

**Area-context-adjusted core specification.** The conceptually planned density/income block was implemented **after Phase 9** once sources were audited; it was not the original Phase 7-frozen baseline. M0_CTX/ME_CTX/MW_CTX/MEW_CTX add only log gross-polygon population density and disposable income to their matching base models, on the same sample and folds. Their scientific role is the **area-context-adjusted core specification**. Geographic XGBoost ME_CTX and MW_CTX gain only about +0.00004 and −0.00056 RMSE relative to M0_CTX. Copenhagen-only MW_CTX gains about +0.00091, but improves only five of ten district folds. Thus stable additional predictive information beyond observed area socioeconomic context is **not supported**. [Matched context comparison](../outputs/tables/context_comparison.csv); [context performance](../outputs/tables/context_augmented_performance.csv); [Copenhagen-only sensitivity](../outputs/tables/copenhagen_only_context_sensitivity.csv).

**Robustness and limits.** Apartment/condo, reviewed, review-count, price-trim and 1.5 km block checks mostly retain a small original MW-versus-M0 gain; 500 m buffered LOAO reverses the XGBoost gain and 1,000 m leaves it near zero. High-price-completeness batches and union-restricted destination endpoints preserve the *base-specification* pattern, but neither removes context attenuation or missing-outcome selection. The two context fields vary across only ten Copenhagen districts plus a **Frederiksberg municipality-level proxy**; City and national income definitions and polygon-density denominators are not fully harmonised. HC3 listing-level intervals do not create 12,412 independent area observations. [Phase 10 robustness](../outputs/tables/phase10/robustness_summary.csv); [observability comparison](../outputs/tables/price_observability_comparison.csv); [boundary comparison](../outputs/tables/boundary_sensitivity_comparison.csv); [context source audit](postcompletion_01_context_audit.md).

**Conclusion.** Accessibility modestly improves the frozen base comparison, but a stable incremental gain beyond measured area socioeconomic context is not shown. Network access has no consistent predictive superiority over the matched Euclidean measures. No causal interpretation is warranted.

## RQ3 — How does spatial separation change predictive evaluation?

**Question.** How well do models transfer when held-out observations are separated in different ways?

**Four distinct designs.** Saved random five-fold CV is the **spatially interspersed benchmark**. Saved 1.5 km whole-block five-fold CV is **non-administrative spatial robustness** (53 blocks allocated outcome-blind to five folds; blocks are not individual folds). LOAO is the **primary unseen-area geographic test**: all ten official Copenhagen city districts and Frederiksberg municipality are each held out once. Buffered LOAO removes training listings within 500 m or 1,000 m of the unchanged held-out polygon. No area or block assignment was regenerated. [Validation design table](../outputs/tables/final/table_validation_designs.csv); [saved-fold report](pre_freeze_validation_visualisation.md).

**Quantitative result.** Frozen XGBoost MW pooled RMSE is 0.31205 under random CV, 0.32263 under 1.5 km block CV, 0.32976 under LOAO, and 0.33629/0.33965 with 500/1,000 m LOAO training buffers. The MW-versus-M0 improvement is +0.00840 random, +0.00422 block, +0.00301 LOAO, −0.00056 at 500 m and +0.00026 at 1,000 m. Area-level gains are heterogeneous. [Validation performance](../outputs/tables/final/table_validation_performance.csv); [Phase 9 fold scores](../outputs/tables/phase09/fold_performance.csv); [Phase 10 buffer diagnostics](../outputs/tables/phase10/buffer_train_loss.csv).

**Conclusion and limit.** Prediction degrades or changes as the held-out task becomes more geographically separated, and the incremental walking-access gain is not equally stable under all designs. This is **not** proof that every random–geographic error difference is leakage: the test geography, training sample size, predictor support and the unseen Frederiksberg municipality category also change. Neither random folds nor block folds guarantee a minimum train–test distance at adjacent boundaries.
